Shipping & returns

Clear, simple delivery and return information for your order.

Delivery

Getting it home.

In-stock orders are prepared within 3–5 business days. Standard delivery is complimentary on orders over $150. Furniture delivery is arranged by appointment in select areas.

Tracking your order

We’ll email tracking details once your order leaves our studio. Furniture delivery teams will contact you to arrange a convenient time.

Returns

Make sure it feels right.

Unused in-stock items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. Contact us with your order number to get started. Made-to-order and final-sale items are not eligible for return.
