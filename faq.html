Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers to help make your experience easy.

Orders & delivery
When will my order ship?

In-stock pieces usually leave our studio within 3–5 business days. You’ll receive tracking details by email.

Do you offer free shipping?

Standard delivery is complimentary on orders over $150 within the contiguous United States.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible. We’ll do our best to help before your order ships.

Returns & products
What is your return policy?

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Some made-to-order items may have different terms.

How do I care for my furniture?

Use a soft, dry cloth for regular dusting and follow the care notes included with each piece.
